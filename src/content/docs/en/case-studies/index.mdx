Theoretical knowledge must be grounded in real-world application. This final part examines how industry leaders implement distributed systems principles to solve massive-scale engineering challenges, from distributed event streaming and NoSQL databases to hyperscale microservices and real-time analytics architectures.

### Chapters

* **[13.1. Messaging Platform: Apache Kafka](/distributed-systems/en/case-studies/apache-kafka/)**: A deep dive into the architecture that powers modern event streaming, focusing on storage optimization, consensus mechanisms, and exactly-once semantics.
* **[13.2. Distributed Key-Value Store: DynamoDB / Cassandra](/distributed-systems/en/case-studies/dynamodb-cassandra/)**: Examines how leading NoSQL databases achieve single-digit millisecond latency at scale through consistent hashing, specialized data models, and conflict resolution.
* **[13.3. Microservices at Scale: Netflix, Uber, Airbnb](/distributed-systems/en/case-studies/microservices-at-scale/)**: Analyzes the architectural evolutions, domain-oriented designs, and cultural practices (like chaos engineering and blameless postmortems) of cloud-native pioneers.
* **[13.4. Real-Time Analytics Platforms](/distributed-systems/en/case-studies/realtime-analytics/)**: Investigates architectures designed to query massive datasets with sub-second latency, covering real-time OLAP, Data Mesh principles, and ML serving.
