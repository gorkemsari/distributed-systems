Modern distributed systems treat physical infrastructure as an abstract pool of compute resources. This chapter explores the foundational architecture of Kubernetes, detailing its primary workload resources, the intelligence behind its scheduling engine, and how it automates complex operational tasks at scale.

### Topics Covered

* **[9.1.1. Pod, Deployment, StatefulSet, DaemonSet: When to Use Which](/distributed-systems/en/kubernetes/fundamentals/pod-deployment-statefulset/)**: Explains the core workload APIs and how to choose the right abstraction for stateless web servers, persistent databases, or node-level background agents.
* **[9.1.2. The Kubernetes Scheduler: The Mechanics of Placement Decisions](/distributed-systems/en/kubernetes/fundamentals/kubernetes-scheduler/)**: Details the multi-step algorithm the control plane uses to evaluate nodes and assign pods based on resource limits and constraints.
* **[9.1.3. etcd: The Heart of Cluster State](/distributed-systems/en/kubernetes/fundamentals/etcd-cluster-state/)**: Deep dives into the highly available, distributed key-value store that acts as the ultimate source of truth for the entire Kubernetes cluster.
* **[9.1.4. Horizontal Pod Autoscaler (HPA) and KEDA: Event-Driven Scaling](/distributed-systems/en/kubernetes/fundamentals/hpa-keda/)**: Explores scaling workloads dynamically based on traditional CPU/memory utilization versus external metrics like message queue depth.
* **[9.1.5. Node Affinity, Taints and Tolerations: Fine-Grained Placement](/distributed-systems/en/kubernetes/fundamentals/node-affinity-taints/)**: Covers the advanced scheduling constraints required to repel pods from specific nodes or attract them to specialized hardware (e.g., GPUs or isolated SSD nodes).
* **[9.1.6. The Operator Pattern: Extending Kubernetes](/distributed-systems/en/kubernetes/fundamentals/operator-pattern/)**: Examines how to build custom controllers that encode human operational knowledge (like running database backups and failovers) directly into the Kubernetes API.
