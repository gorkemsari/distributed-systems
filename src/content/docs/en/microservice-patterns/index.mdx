Building a distributed system is more than just splitting code into smaller pieces; it requires a disciplined approach to service boundaries, data ownership, and communication. This part explores the architectural blueprints and repeatable patterns used to design, decompose, and manage microservices at scale, ensuring they remain independent, evolvable, and resilient.



### Chapters

* **[6.1. Service Decomposition Strategies](/distributed-systems/en/microservice-patterns/service-decomposition/)**: Focuses on the "how" and "where" of slicing a system using Domain-Driven Design (DDD) and organizational alignment.
* **[6.2. Structural Patterns](/distributed-systems/en/microservice-patterns/structural-patterns/)**: Covers modular deployment models like Sidecars and Ambassadors to handle cross-cutting concerns outside the application logic.
* **[6.3. Service Discovery](/distributed-systems/en/microservice-patterns/service-discovery/)**: Details how services locate one another in dynamic, ephemeral network environments.
* **[6.4. Data Management Patterns](/distributed-systems/en/microservice-patterns/data-management-patterns/)**: Examines the complexities of data sovereignty, polyglot persistence, and cross-service queries.
* **[6.5. Event Sourcing and CQRS](/distributed-systems/en/microservice-patterns/event-sourcing-cqrs/)**: Explores advanced state management where every change is an immutable event and read models are decoupled from writes.
