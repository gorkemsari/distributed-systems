Securing a distributed system requires more than just a strong perimeter firewall. When hundreds of microservices communicate across dynamic, ephemeral networks, security must be embedded deeply into every layer. This part, referenced in **index_52.mdx**, explores modern security paradigms, from identity and access management to zero-trust networks, cryptographic secrets management, and supply chain integrity.

### Chapters

* **[11.1. Authentication and Authorization](/distributed-systems/en/security/authentication-authorization/)**: Focuses on how users and services prove their identity and how to strictly control what actions they are permitted to perform.
* **[11.2. Zero Trust Architecture](/distributed-systems/en/security/zero-trust/)**: Explores the modern security model that assumes the internal network is already compromised, requiring continuous verification for every request.
* **[11.3. Secrets Management and Encryption](/distributed-systems/en/security/secrets-management/)**: Details how to safely store, rotate, and access sensitive credentials, secure data cryptographically, and protect the software supply chain against tampering.
